# Homepage (app/pages/index.vue)

Scope: the homepage, rebuilt from scratch (owner, 2026-10-22: "fully start from scratch the whole page layout, keep the data only"). Mode: Persuade. Audiences: makers across Kerala and visitors/families, equally.
Action: Get Involved (/interestform); visitors learn date (5-6 Feb 2027), place (The Grounds @ Chakola Mill, Kochi) and free entry.
Proof: global network figures, organisers, the domain list, live drawings of making with people doing it. No invented claims.
Constraints: every existing string kept; one countdown only (the 3D machine); no people in the hero; people only where making happens.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Make Some Noise meets the maker's bench. A loud campaign poster opens the page, then the story moves through whole-section colour fields, each holding real making in motion. It refuses the stacked-card event landing and the old drawing-sheet chrome (sheet heads, title blocks, dot grids, routes).
OWN-WORLD: Dela Gothic One headlines and Inter text. Warm yellow #FFE86C and electric blue #2B19D9 as section-sized fields on a paper #F6F6F3 / ink #222 base. Maker red for the one action, cyan structural only. Live three.js figures sit directly on the fields.
STORY: Hero (where are the makers?) / What Maker Faire is (Bay Area 2006 to Kochi 2027) / Why Kochi + the cheena vala / The faire floor (domains, carpenter, maker's bench close-ups) / Make some noise / Three ways in / When and where (the machine counts down) / 2027 and beyond.
FIRST VIEWPORT: yellow field. Blue Dela headline left over two lines, the definition and the red Get Involved below. Robots playing on a test mat fill the right half. A blue facts strip along the bottom: dates, venue (with map), free entry.
FORM: campaign poster + colour-field story, user-pinned (no roll).
SIGNATURE: the robots are playable (grab, fling, they crash and get back up); the maker's bench zooms detail by detail as you scroll.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
